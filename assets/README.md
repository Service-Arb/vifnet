## [profile_images](./profile_images/)

### Categories

- `before_after_<subject>` — visible before/after or comparison imagery; include the room, fixture, furniture, or scene.
- `final_<subject>_<number>` — finished portfolio imagery; describe the visible room, property, furniture, or feature.
- `staff_in_<setting>_<number>` — a visible staff member working in the named setting.
- `protected_<space>_<number>` — a visible cleaning-service scene where furnishings or surfaces are covered or protected during work.
- `team_<subject>_<number>` — a visible team or group portrait.
- `in_progress_<subject>` — a cleaning job mid-work, with equipment visible but no staff.

Use lowercase snake_case, keep the functional category first, and add a numeric suffix only when multiple images share the same subject.
